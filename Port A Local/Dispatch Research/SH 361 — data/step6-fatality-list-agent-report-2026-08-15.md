## (3) FARS pull — method

## (6) Contradictions / caveats on "17 fatalities / ~900 crashes over 10 years"
- FARS does not reach 17 for the corridor: FARS 2014–2024 = 12 fatal crashes / 14 deaths (2015–2024: 11 / 13). As of Dec 2024 (KRIS's 17) the corridor tally was 14. FARS deaths on ALL of SH 361 within Nueces County (corridor + Port A town + Aransas Pass/causeway side) for 2015–2024 = exactly 17 (13 + 1 + 1 + 2) — the "17" likely reflects a county-wide 361 query. Not verified — TxDOT/CRIS query definition unknown.
- The reported series shifts: KRIS Jul 27, 2023 "ten fatal accidents since 2013" (FARS corridor to that date: 9); KRIS May 15, 2024 "10th fatal accident since 2014" (FARS: 11th); KIII Aug 18, 2025 "15th fatal accident in the last 10 years" (FARS+news corridor Aug 2015–Aug 2025: 11; from Jan 2014: 13); KRIS May 4, 2026 "15 people killed 2016–2026" (corridor 2016–May 2026: 13–14). None match each other or FARS exactly; all attributed to TxDOT/DPS without a stated segment definition.
- KIII headline "15th fatality" vs body "15th fatal accident" — deaths ≠ crashes (two double-fatal crashes in 2022 and 2024).
- DPS Sgt. Mallory's 2024 "two fatalities" (as of Aug 13, 2024) undercounts deaths (Stephen Pryor 5/14, Margaret Pryor 6/2, Salinas 8/9 = 3) but matches fatal crashes (2); his 2020–2023 figures (0, 0, 3, 3) match FARS corridor deaths exactly.
- "~900 crashes/10 yrs" is plausible but unconfirmed as corridor-only: DPS/CRIS per-year totals (2020: 72; 2021: 109; 2022: 88; 2023: 94; 2024 to mid-Aug: 55) average ~90/yr — consistent with ~900/decade, but likely Nueces-County-wide 361.
- Access-blocked sources: kiiitv.com (Akamai 403, incl. Wayback captures after ~2023) — KIII items cite Wayback copies (2022, May 2024) or syndication/snippets; caller.com paywalled — the one Caller-Times piece is quoted from TxDOT's official meeting summary. South Jetty read entirely via Wayback (60 articles fetched; several 2016–2019 print stories not archived, hence two FARS crashes with no press match).
