## Notes on method / how to read the table
- **Coordinates** are the OSM node where each side street/driveway meets the SH 361 centerline (Overpass query of every OSM way sharing a node with the `ref=TX 361` ways, 2026-08-15); OSM ways with no name were reverse-geocoded (Nominatim) and matched to NCAD situs addresses. Google Maps could not be scripted; where I inferred which property an unnamed driveway serves, the row says "probable"/"VERIFY".
- **Side**: E = Gulf/beach side, W = bay side, computed from the highway bearing.
- **Opened/platted**: where no press/developer source exists, "NCAD" = Nueces CAD eSearch (esearch.nuecescad.net) sampling of 2-3 parcels per subdivision/condo (lowest property IDs + highest value) — reported as *earliest sampled improvement year-built* and *earliest deed date*; these are lower bounds on "first built", not plat dates. Nueces CAD's ProPAccess (propaccess.trueautomation.com) returned HTTP 504 all session; eSearch worked without CAPTCHA (`/search/shouldUseRecaptcha` = false).
- **Control**: "SIGNALIZED" only where OSM has `highway=traffic_signals` (Access Rd 1A and PR 22 — the only two on the corridor); turn-lane notes come from OSM `turn:lanes` tags and TxDOT's 2024 description; "temporary" signals = Port Aransas PD's portable lights (Spring Break 2026, at Access Rd 1 and 1-B).
- **South Jetty** was fetched via Wayback (`web.archive.org/web/<ts>id_/`); recent (2021-2025) stories are paywalled after the lede — quotes below are limited to what the archived pages expose.
