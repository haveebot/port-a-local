## THESIS DISCIPLINE (the honest version)

The vulnerable version: *"developments are causing the crashes."* TxDOT's #1 factor is speeding and DUI is in the top five — a piece ignoring that gets discredited.

The defensible version, which the records actually support:
> A two-lane undivided highway built as a **conduit** — a beginning and an end — is being converted into a road with **driveways**, without the access-management engineering that a road with driveways requires. Driver error (speed, alcohol) is constant everywhere; what a corridor's **design** determines is whether an ordinary error becomes a fender-bender or a head-on fatality. Every new access point adds conflict opportunities to a road with no median to absorb them. TxDOT named the growth-and-safety problem on Mustang Island in 2011, applied the remedy to the town in 2016–17, and has the island's remedy scheduled for 2030 — contingent on funding.

## PIECE ARCHITECTURE — proposed after session 2 (Winston decides; per [[feedback_dispatch_two_part_series]])

TxDOT applied the remedy to the town in 2016–17, flagged the island in 2018, and scheduled it for 2030 "contingent on funding."
