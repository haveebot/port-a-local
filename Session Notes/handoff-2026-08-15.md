## THE FINDINGS THE PIECE RESTS ON (all reproducible from `Dispatch Research/SH 361 — data/`)

- TxDOT corridor (Beach Access Rd 1 → PR 22), 2016–Aug 15 2026: **421 crashes · 14 fatal crashes · 17 deaths · 18 serious-injury crashes**. (CRIS has 12 fatal/14 deaths; the Aug 10 and Aug 14 crashes are not ingested yet and are counted from police accounts.)
- **76.6 crashes per 100M VMT vs the Texas rural two-lane 100.5 — but 2.58 deaths vs the state's 1.38.** One crash in 30 kills here; statewide ~1 in 140.
- **Every fatal crash since 2016 is north of Access Road 3** — the one stretch TxDOT programmed and funded first (212 crashes, **zero deaths** there in ten and a half years). The ~8 unprogrammed middle miles hold 9 of 14 fatal crashes and 12 of 17 deaths.
- TxDOT's widening is two end pieces: Access Rd 3→PR 22 ($60M, let 2032, in the UTP) and Access Rd 1→Mariners Dr ($54M, let 2036, not in the UTP). Verified from the agency's own project tracker, saved as JSON.
- The corridor contains its own before/after: TxDOT proposed the town boulevard in **2011** on growth-and-safety grounds, built it **2016–17 for $5.8M** (TDLR), and that curbed-median section has the island's highest crash rate and killed nobody 2016–2025.
- Beach Access Road 1-B: unanimous **3/21/2024** easement swap with "a stoplight also is planned" → **bid April 2025 with one stop sign** → opened 3/2/2026 → portable police-operated light for Spring Break → April 2026 deferral. Signals on SH 361 need a TxDOT warrant; none is warranted on the 15 miles.
- Impairment in **10 of 15** fatal crashes since 2016 — and, per investigators, neither of this week's.
